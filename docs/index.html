Spillway Designer

Ogee spillway, free or gated, with chute, hydraulic jump and stilling basin

Rating curve

Preliminary-design tool. Coefficient tables are approximate readings of USACE and USBR charts, the flow is one-dimensional with no air entrainment, and tailwater is an input. The reference calculation is in notebook/spillway_design.ipynb; this page matches it to six significant figures (see tests/).

Check results against EM 1110-2-1603 and USBR EM-25 before using them for a real structure.
